Infinity Stones Relay

Race • Pass • Conquer

Setup
01 / 03
4 teams7 players each7 boxes
  • There are 4 teams of 7 players, each with its own lane.
  • Each lane has 7 boxes in a row and a finish line after the last box.
  • Each team gets the same large pile of bottles at its start line.
How to Play
02 / 03
  1. On “Go!”, Player 1 grabs bottles, runs to Box 1, stands them upright, runs back and tags Player 2.
  2. Players 2–6 do the same, filling one box each (Box 2 through Box 6).
  3. Player 7 takes the remaining bottles, runs past all 6 boxes and places them in Box 7.
  4. The team shouts “Done!” when Player 7 puts down the last bottle.
Rules
03 / 03
  • Only one runner per team at a time. Wait for the tag before going.
  • Bottles must stand upright. If a bottle falls in a box or beyond the finish line, stand it up again before leaving.
  • Do not touch another team’s boxes or bottles.

Marvel: 30 Seconds to Assemble

Watch the scene • Name the movie • Name the hero

Setup
01 / 03
4 teams5 players each30 sec scene
  • There are 4 teams of 5 players each.
  • Each team comes forward to watch a Marvel movie scene.
  • Each scene plays for 30 seconds only.
  • Teams must guess the movie name and the character name.
How to Play
02 / 03
  1. On “Go!”, all 5 players of the first team come forward.
  2. A Marvel movie scene plays for 30 seconds.
  3. All 5 players can discuss the movie and character.
  4. After the scene ends, the team has 10 seconds to give its final answer.
  5. Each team gets only one chance to answer each scene.
  6. The same process continues for the remaining teams.
Rules
03 / 03
  • No mobile phones, internet searches or outside help.
  • No hints or assistance from the audience or other teams.
  • Scenes play only once. No replay or extra time.
  • Once an answer is submitted, it cannot be changed.
  • Answers after the time limit will not be accepted.
  • Shouting answers or disturbing others may cause disqualification.
  • Any cheating results in immediate disqualification.

House Rules

A few simple rules so everyone has a great day.

01

Be On Time

Entry opens at 08:00 AM sharp. Heroes don't keep the squad waiting.

02

Wear Your Colors

Team jerseys on all day. Show everyone which side you're on.

03

Play Fair, Play Hard

Compete with everything you've got. The referee's call is final.

04

Stay Hydrated

Drink water between games. Even Thor takes a break.

05

No One Left Behind

Look out for your teammates on and off the field.

Have Fun

It's an outing, not the Avengers final. Enjoy the day.
