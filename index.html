Read Before You Play

House Rules

A few simple rules so everyone has a great day.

01

Be On Time

We roll out at 07:30 AM sharp. Heroes don't keep the squad waiting.

02

Wear Your Colors

Team jerseys on all day. Show everyone which side you're on.

03

Play Fair, Play Hard

Compete with everything you've got. The referee's call is final.

04

Stay Hydrated

Drink water between games. Even Thor takes a break.

05

No One Left Behind

Look out for your teammates on and off the field.

06

Have Fun

It's an outing, not the Avengers final. Enjoy the day.

⚠️

Disclaimer

Taking part in any sport is optional. Play within your limits and tell an organizer if you feel unwell or get hurt. Please keep track of your own belongings. The schedule may change on the day.
